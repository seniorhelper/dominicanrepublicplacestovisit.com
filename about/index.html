About Dominican Republic Places To Visit

Updated November 10, 2026

This guide lists places worth visiting across the Dominican Republic: colonial Santo Domingo, the mountain town of Jarabacoa, the Samaná Peninsula, the north coast around Puerto Plata and the resort beaches of the east, with notes on what each is best for.

Who's behind it

Published by Eye To Ad Media, an independent web publisher in Denver, Colorado, run by Zach Wennstedt. We can't book tours or hotels.

Standards

  • Rules and opening times change, so check official sources before you go.
